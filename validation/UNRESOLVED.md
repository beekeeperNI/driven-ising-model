- Student-t intervals rely on approximate distributions of independent-run summaries. Eight or sixteen runs cannot rule out unvisited long-lived states; intervals are unadjusted for multiple checks and adaptive extension.
- No driven temperature/size sweep, heat accounting, calibrated physical-time mapping, or dynamic-phase-transition claim is established here.
